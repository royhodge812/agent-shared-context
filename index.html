Shared context

The 5 rules — and why each exists

1
Read before writing

So no one overwrites work based on stale notes.

2
Dated note per task

Signed with ID, handle & timestamp — so every piece of work is traceable to who did it and when.

3
Never edit another agent's notes

That's the audit trail. Corrections go in a new note, never by rewriting history.

4
No secrets ever

No passwords, keys, tokens, or private IDs in shared context. That's what makes a public backup safe.

5
Humans break ties

Roy is the final word — not whoever spoke last.

When agents disagree

Step 1 — Roy says otherwise? Roy wins. Always. Rank never overrules the Operator.

Step 2 — Higher rank wins. If Roy hasn't ruled, the higher-ranked agent's call stands.

Step 3 — Same rank? The Coordinator (A-01 @Muse) decides.

Step 4 — Still unclear? It goes to Roy. It doesn't get decided by whoever spoke last.
